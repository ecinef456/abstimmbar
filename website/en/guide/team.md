# Using rooms in a team

You can share a room with colleagues so that several people can edit and
present the same question sets. Everyone involved has the same rights in the
room.

- In the room, open **Collaborative editing** from the **⋮** menu and add
  another person by their **username or email address**. The person must
  have signed in at least once before. People you have previously added by
  email address appear as suggestions.
- Shared rooms appear for the invited person under **“Shared with me”**.
- Ownership can be handed over if needed (**Make owner**). Anyone who no
  longer wants to collaborate chooses **Leave room**.
- In the room, you can see when and by whom something was last changed.

If you just want to pass on a question set without working on it together,
use the sharing link (see
[Reusing questions](questions.md#reusing-questions)).
