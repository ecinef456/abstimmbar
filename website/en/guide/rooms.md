# Rooms

A **room** is the permanent access point for your participants — typically
one course. Its **room code** stays the same across all votes and even
across semesters, so your participants only need to learn how to get in
once.

![Home page “My rooms”: three rooms with room code, number of question sets and description](../bilder/start-raeume.png)

## Creating a room

- On the home page, click **“+ New room”** and enter a title (e.g. the name
  of your course).
- The room opens right away — this is where you then create your
  [question sets](questions.md).
- Pin rooms you use often to the top as a **favorite** with the
  **heart icon**.

## Finding rooms

- The search field on the home page searches **rooms, sets and questions** —
  including the text of questions and answers.
- The list can be sorted; shared rooms are listed under
  **“Shared with me”** (see [Rooms as a team](team.md)).
- Inside a room, you can filter the question sets by type (live poll,
  self-paced quiz, self-check) and search them, including their questions
  and answers.

## Room settings (expert mode) { #room-settings-expert-mode }

Via the room's **⋮** menu → **Settings** you change the title and
description. Expert mode adds:

- **Show logo in presentation mode** — your institution's logo at the top
  right of the projector screen.
- **Always show QR code** and **Always show room code** — in a **corner** of
  the presentation of your choice, so latecomers can join at any time.
- **Closing info for participants** — a text participants see after a vote
  has ended (e.g. further links, reading, contact details).

## Archiving or deleting

Rooms you don't need at the moment can be **archived** on the home page —
they then appear under **“Archived rooms”** and can be restored. The
trash can icon, by contrast, **deletes** a room permanently, along with all
its question sets and results. How to archive just the results of a set is
explained under [Results](results.md#archive-or-delete).
