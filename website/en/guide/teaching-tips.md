# Quick tips for teaching

- Short, unambiguous questions work best — one key point per question.
- Use polls to activate your audience (check prior knowledge, gauge progress,
  spark discussion).
- Create the room once per course and reuse it for the whole semester — the
  code stays the same.
- Keep the QR code visible at all times so latecomers can join too.
- [Before-and-after questions](questions.md#before-after-questions-expert-mode)
  make learning effects visible.
- Word cloud, mind map and four-field matrix are well suited for collecting
  ideas. With mind map and four-field matrix, a subsequent rating phase gives
  you a prioritisation right away.
- For follow-up at home: a [self-check](runs.md#self-check) with a permanent
  link, e.g. linked in your LMS.
