# Recorded lectures (expert mode)

If you record your session with an external tool, switch on **Recording
mode** in the question set before presenting (the checkbox above the list of
questions; not available for self-checks). In the presentation, **each
question then gets its own QR code**, which ends up in the recording. Anyone
watching the recording later can scan it and vote on exactly the question
shown, after the fact.

!!! tip
    Recording mode only applies to this presentation. A small notice on the
    projector shows that it is switched on.
