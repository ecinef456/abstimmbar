# Sign-in and modes

## Signing in

Sign in with your usual university account (single sign-on). You can also
add Abstimmbar directly as an activity in your learning platform (LTI 1.3;
e.g. Moodle; Stud.IP as soon as it supports LTI 1.3) and skip the separate
login — [In the learning platform](lms.md) explains how.
Rooms created from within the LMS are marked **“From LMS”** on the home
page.

Your participants, on the other hand, need **no account**: they join
anonymously via a QR code or the room code (see
[Inviting participants](inviting-participants.md)).

## Simple mode and expert mode

The first time you use it, the interface starts in **simple mode** with the
most important features. Use the mode switch at the top right (“Simple” /
“Expert”) to switch to **expert mode** with all settings at any time. Your
choice is saved.

Among other things, only expert mode shows you:

- **Sections** in question sets and **before/after questions**
  (see [Creating questions](questions.md)),
- **Duplicate**, **Export (JSON)**, **Copy set (share link)** and
  **Archive results** in a question set's menu,
- **AI assistance** (if your institution has enabled it,
  see [AI assistance](ai-assistance.md)),
- **bilingual content** (German/English, see
  [Bilingual content](bilingual-content.md)),
- **recording mode** (see [Recorded lectures](recorded-lectures.md)),
- the **room settings** for the logo, a permanently displayed QR/room code
  and the **Closing info for participants**,
- moderating word clouds, free text and mind maps directly on the projector.

The [tour](guided-tour.md) adapts to your mode.

## Language and appearance

In the **user menu** (your initial at the top right) you choose the interface
language (German or English) and the **Appearance**: light, dark or matching
your system. The language is saved to your account. Your participants can
switch the language of the participant page themselves, independently of
this — more in [Bilingual content](bilingual-content.md).
