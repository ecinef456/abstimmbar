# In the learning platform (LTI)

Abstimmbar can be integrated into learning platforms via the **LTI 1.3**
standard — e.g. Moodle; Stud.IP as soon as LTI 1.3 is available there. The
technical setup is done once by the administrators of your learning platform
(see [LMS integration (LTI 1.3)](../lti.md)). After that, you can use
Abstimmbar in your courses like an activity.

## Adding it to a course

1. In your course, add an **“External tool”** activity and choose the
   Abstimmbar connection set up by your administrators.
2. **“Select content”** opens Abstimmbar's selection page: choose an
   **existing question set** from the course room, or enter a title to create
   a **new** one.
3. Save the activity. You can create several activities — for example one per
   question set.

## What happens when it's opened

**For you as a teacher:**

- You are **signed in automatically** — no separate login needed. Depending on
  the setup, this is the same account as when you sign in with your
  university username.
- The first time you open it from a course, Abstimmbar creates a **room for
  the course** (title = course title) and links it permanently to the course.
  Every teacher who opens Abstimmbar from the course becomes a member of this
  room — so you work on the same room as a [team](team.md).
- You land directly in the selected question set (or in the course room) and
  can present from there. On your start page, the room is labelled
  **“From LMS”**.

**For students:**

- They are taken **without an account and anonymously** to the participant
  page of the course room — just as via QR code or room code. Abstimmbar
  learns nothing about them from the LMS that would be stored with their
  votes.
- There they see what you are **currently presenting** in this room or have
  started as a self-paced quiz. If nothing is running, the page says “The vote
  has not started yet.” — it starts by itself as soon as you begin.
- If the activity is linked to a **self-check**, students land directly in
  that self-check — provided you have
  [published](set-types.md#self-check) it.
- If students open the activity before a teacher has opened it once, they get
  a notice that the course is not linked yet.

!!! tip
    Open a new activity once yourself before your students use it — that way
    the course room is created.
