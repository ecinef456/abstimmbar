# Privacy

Abstimmbar is **designed for anonymity**: participants don't need an account,
and voters' IP addresses are not stored. So you can use the tool with
confidence to engage your audience and to get honest feedback — even on
sensitive topics.

- **Participants** stay anonymous; their device only receives a random
  identifier, so that it can't vote twice, for example, and can show “My
  answers”.
- **AI features** are optional, must be enabled by your institution and run
  via the service it operates or has commissioned. Only content (questions,
  answers, terms, cards) is sent to the model — no names or other data about
  participants. Please note the information under
  [AI assistance](ai-assistance.md).
- Use of the [guided tour](guided-tour.md) and of the mode is only recorded as
  anonymous statistics.

You'll find the privacy policy of your instance in the footer of the
application.
