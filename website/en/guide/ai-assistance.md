# AI assistance (expert mode)

AI assistance appears only in **expert mode**, and only if your institution
has enabled the AI features. Your institution also decides which model is
used behind the scenes — it may well be a locally hosted one. The AI
**supports you, it doesn't replace anything**: every suggestion is optional,
and you always decide for yourself what to use.

## While creating

- **Suggest distractors** — plausible wrong answers for choice questions.
  *Example:* you type in the question and the correct answer; the AI adds
  convincing distractors that you can accept or discard one by one.
- **Rephrase question** — clearer or shorter versions of your question.
- **Generate questions from document** — via **From document** in the set,
  from PDF, PowerPoint (PPTX) or LibreOffice (ODP). *Example:* you upload
  your slides, pick a question level, and the AI suggests single-choice,
  multiple-choice and free-text questions.

## During the presentation

You switch these features on per question in the editor (**Enable AI
features** or the question's AI toggles); on the projector you reach them
via the **star** on the right-hand edge. Details are in
[Presenting](presenting.md).

- **Optimize word cloud** — merge spelling variants and synonyms, group by
  topic.
- **Optimise mind map** — merge duplicate terms, unify the language,
  restructure; with a preview before anything is applied.
- **Group matrix cards** — stack identical or similar cards under a summary
  card.
- **Evaluate free text (live)** — automatically sort open answers into 2–5
  categories of your choice, grade them against a **model solution**, or
  condense them into **key statements**. If you like, each person sees the
  evaluation of their own answer.

## When evaluating

- **Create short report** — a compact summary of the results on the
  [results page](results.md).

!!! warning "Privacy note"
    When evaluating free text, optimizing a word cloud, mind map or matrix,
    and creating a short report, the **answers** (not the people) are sent to
    your institution's AI service. Use these features with appropriate care.
    More on this under [Privacy](privacy.md).
